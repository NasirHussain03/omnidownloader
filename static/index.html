Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about downloading videos with OmniDownloader.

Is OmniDownloader free to use? ▼

Yes, OmniDownloader is completely free with no limits on downloads. There are no subscriptions, registration requirements, or hidden fees.

Can I download YouTube videos in 1080p Full HD and 4K? ▼

Yes! OmniDownloader supports extraction of all available resolutions provided by the source video, including 4K (2160p), 1440p (2K), 1080p Full HD, and 720p HD.

How do I save Instagram Reels to my phone gallery? ▼

Copy the link to the Reel from Instagram, paste it into OmniDownloader on your mobile browser (Safari or Chrome), and click Download. The MP4 video will be saved directly to your phone.

Does OmniDownloader store or keep copies of downloaded videos? ▼

No. OmniDownloader respects your privacy. We do not store downloaded files on our servers. All downloads are streamed directly from the original content delivery networks to your device.
